The seventh NVForever pre-release: everything merged since v0.6.0 (2026-11-04), about 320
pull requests. The main news is **FSR 3.1 upscaling with frame generation**, a **rebuilt
settings page with UI scale and quality presets**, **classic V.A.T.S.**, **NVForever in your
install's language**, **the xNVSE-family function lists completed** (JIP LN, JohnnyGuitar,
ShowOff, SUP and Lutana), **optional reports to help fix NVForever**, and a long list of
combat, AI, save and quest fixes checked against the original.

**You need your own copy of Fallout: New Vegas (1.4.0.525, base game + all DLC).** No game
data is included. Unpack anywhere outside the game folder and run `nvforever.exe` (or
`nvforever.cmd`; `./nvforever.sh` on Linux). The first start asks for your game folder and
remembers it, and the game opens on the main menu. `README-release.md` in the archive
explains the rest. NVForever never writes into the game folder or
`Documents\My Games\FalloutNV`; its own home is `Documents\My Games\NVForever`.

**Reports (new, opt-in):** the first start of this release asks once whether NVForever may
send a report after each session (build, hardware and settings, mod list, how it ran, the
game's log, crashes). Nothing is sent unless you choose **Send reports**; no personal
details and no saves are sent, and every report is first written to
`Documents\My Games\NVForever\reports` where you can read it. You can turn it off, or delete
what was sent, in Settings → Gameplay → Reports. What is sent:
`docs/design/player-reports.md` in the repository.

## New since v0.6.0

### Graphics and performance
- **FSR 3.1** temporal upscaling on Vulkan, with **frame generation**, **AMD Anti-Lag**, masks against ghosting and shimmer, and cheaper passes (#1771, #1960, #1961, #1962, #1963, #1964, #2071, #2076, #2086). Thanks to h9x000.
- **Quality presets** read from your install's own files, and an Enhanced effects option (#2025, #2049).
- **Water** colour follows depth below the surface (#1789). **Strip bloom** reworked so the Lucky 38 and Tops signs read and the night sky stays dark (#1810).
- **Interiors** hide meshes wholly behind occlusion planes, room culling follows linked rooms (TTW's Vault 101), and interior lights shine the right way (#1774, #1888, #1818).
- **Terrain** draws each texture layer only where it paints and joins cleanly along cell and quadrant edges (#1889, #2096).
- **Faster starts** (the script build in under half the time), **smoother big battles** (detection, battle AI and ragdoll solver costs cut, stuck actors held instead of swept every step) and **fewer stalls when actors appear** (#1808, #1734, #1802, #1824, #1831, #1816, #1768, #1800).
- Less work each frame for particles off screen, distant sounds, faces, idle water and the night sky (#1885, #1854, #1855); render-thread clean-up off the main path (#1804, #1890).
- Lower input delay with the frame limiter on (#2076). Better performance on dual-CCD X3D processors (#1843).
- Memory no longer grows from sound files in long sessions, and replaced textures are released (#1699, #2086).

### Settings, HUD and menus
- **Settings page rebuilt**: seven tabs with headings, keyboard search, a list of what you changed, and colour sliders for the Custom HUD colour (#1970, #1979).
- **UI scale** for the HUD and the menus, 50 to 150% (#1766, #1777).
- **Performance overlay** grown from the FPS counter: CPU and GPU usage, frame split and a graph, each with its own switch (#1708, #1947, #2071).
- **Conversations** ease the camera onto the speaker's face and blur the background (can be turned off) (#1748). The "[Tab] Goodbye" hint can be hidden (#1525).
- **Loading screen** in the original's layout with tips and the turning emblem; its picture shows at once (#1749, #1716).
- **Character creator** layout closer to the original's, and the gene projector's creator draws in a lit studio (#1751, #1901). The Vigor Tester lights up and blurs the room (#1750).
- **Pip-Boy**: the device's buttons, glass, needle and knobs work and the screen has the page-change transition (#1528, #2069); Place Marker sets, moves and removes a custom marker (#2054); quest objectives and notes scroll by keys, wheel and stick (#1941); every tab shows button hints for your input device (#1950, #1952); RadAway and limb picking on Status with a controller (#1974); the World Map works in child worldspaces (#1840).
- **Controller**: moves and zooms the Pip-Boy map and fast travels from a World Map marker (#1487, #1823); PlayStation button names and icons (#1752, #1830, #1876); every menu hint shows the button that presses it (#1747). The tutorial names the keys you bound (#2008).
- **Optional head bob** in first person (#1811). Finer mouse sensitivity at low values (#1724). Borderless full screen opens on the monitor you choose (#1680).
- **More menus drawn from the game's own menu files** (behind `[UI] TileMenus`, off by default): container, trait, dialogue, barter, tag skills, S.P.E.C.I.A.L., recipes, vendor repair and the Pip-Boy STATS, ITEMS, repair and DATA pages (#1496, #1527, #1529, #1530, #1794, #1795, #1942, #1943, #1944, #1975, #1945, #1976, #1946, #2032).
- The fast travel question shows its answers as buttons (#1841). Enemy names sit centred on the HUD (#1844).

### Languages
- **NVForever's own text follows your install's language** (Russian, German, French, Italian and Spanish included), main and pause menus too (#1648, #1949, #2103).
- Better fonts on non-English installs (Russian fonts included), language plugins turned on automatically, and Pip-Boy labels from the game's own settings (#1647, #1894, #1900, #1902).

### Combat and gameplay
- **Classic V.A.T.S.** style beside Dead Eye, with a time slowdown slider, plus kill camera, loading and slow-motion physics fixes (#2102). VATS shows every body part's hit chance before you mark one (#1769).
- Grenade launcher rounds leave the muzzle and thrown grenades roll (#1788). NPCs throw grenades at sensible times and fire automatic weapons in bursts with pauses (#1726, #1585).
- NPCs turn onto a new target at the original's pace (#1755) and are less accurate while walking or running (#2146). Silenced weapons are silent to NPCs (#2142). Script-spawned actors attack inside their aggro radius (#1754).
- Melee reach, blast limb shares and the detection light are fitted to the original (#1760, #1757, #1784). Tumbleweeds roll with the wind (#1784).
- Planted live explosives work as in the original: the victim panics, only damaging blasts kill, unseen kills draw no witnesses (#1782). Mines have a longer fuse (#1718).
- Aiming down sights drops during a reload or jam and comes back after (#1643). Your speed follows Speed Mult at once (#1756, #1764).
- Calm creatures play their ordinary idles (#2053). Securitron wheels roll as they move (#1968). Your character speaks with the voice for their sex and age (#2091).
- Leveled loot follows your level (#1654). Seen thefts add infamy (#1675). Level-up skill points pay the half point from odd Intelligence (#2041).

- **Tale of Two Wastelands**: more extender functions the TTW scripts use, start-up checks that report Stewie's Tweaks, ttw_nvse, the 4 GB limit and plugin versions, and the G.O.A.T. classroom seats (#1775, #1883, #1879, #1697).

### Reports and logs

## Fixed since v0.6.0

### Combat
- Disarmed NPCs fight with their fists instead of a T-pose (#1715). Protectrons fire their lasers (#1554). Ceiling turrets see, track and shoot you (#1556). Sentry bots keep their minigun (#1622).
- NPC hits cripple your limbs (#1793). Unarmed weapons show their own hand model (#1783). Robot hit areas are no longer too thin (#1555). Gore pieces are the right size on scaled creatures (#1629).
- Explosions apply damage and Damage Threshold perks correctly, and a grenade's blast is no longer blocked by the surface it landed on (#1671, #1937). Damage past zero Health kills or knocks out essentials (#1655).
- Your magazine never holds more rounds than you carry (#1656). Grenade launcher arc and Incinerator burn fixed (#1781). Kill credit and cause of death are recorded correctly (#1678). NPC weapons no longer wear down from their own attacks (#1733).
- Burning in first person follows your body (#1792). Corpses shot again settle sooner (#1831). Ash and goo piles are not pushed by their body (#2111). Paralysed actors stay down after a fatigue knockout; resurrected actors get their limbs back (#1972, #1971).

### AI and NPCs
- Greeters stop at talking distance (#1670). NPCs a script moves or enables walk instead of running (#1765). Companions keep fighting while following (#1553). NPCs who start a conversation from afar walk up first (Nelson) (#1668).
- Stuck NPCs recover onto their path, and large battles no longer stall on stuck actors (#1560, #1566, #1802). NPCs no longer slip through force fields (HELIOS One's stasis pods), and the robots inside stay dormant (#2019, #2009). Knocked-out robots stand switched off (#1978).
- People and dogs no longer use robot idle markers (the stretched neck) (#1661). Creatures keep off furniture they have no clip for, and NPCs retry busy furniture and sandbox spots (#1559, #1558, #1561). A seated NPC whose AI is reset stands up properly (#2144).
- Mr. House's screen no longer turns to face you and shows the right face for the game's state (#1583, #1689). Conversation head turns stay natural (#1815).

### Saves and loading
- Time of day, weather, ammo type and seated state come back correctly after a load (#1653, #1700, #1688, #1684). Doors rest in their pose after a load (#1690, #1700).
- Planted explosives and C-4 lying in the world survive a save (#1836, #2099). Loading near a map marker no longer discovers it again (#1674). You can no longer save after dying (#1652), and an answered message box no longer blocks saving (#1650).
- Fade transitions caught by a save finish on load (#1701). Loading a save while playing is more reliable (#1695, #1906). Saving is sturdier: odd slot names, two saves at once (#2007).
- Door travel loads the area around you before placing you and no longer carries shots in flight (#1633, #2099).
