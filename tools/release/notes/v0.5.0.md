The fifth NVForever pre-release: everything merged since v0.4.0 (2026-10-21), about 230
pull requests. The main news is **third person, the weapon wheel and hotkeys**, **NPCs that
fight, heal and re-arm like the originals**, **the perks**, **the end credits**, and smoother
streaming with far fewer hitches.

**You need your own copy of Fallout: New Vegas (1.4.0.525, base game + all DLC).** No game
data is included. Unpack anywhere outside the game folder and run `nvforever.exe` (or
`nvforever.cmd`; `./nvforever.sh` on Linux). The first start asks for your game folder and
remembers it, and the game opens on the main menu. `README-release.md` in the archive
explains the rest. NVForever never writes into the game folder or
`Documents\My Games\FalloutNV`; its own home is `Documents\My Games\NVForever`.

Script extender (xNVSE, JIP LN) support is in progress in the repository but is not part of
this release: mods that need a script extender still do not run.

## New since v0.4.0

**Controls, camera and HUD**
- Third person: a detached camera with 360° movement and the weapon put away; the death
  camera keeps its place and turns to the body.
- Hotkeys and a weapon wheel, with the Pip-Boy hotkey panel; Lower Weapon on H; the weapon
  is lowered near walls and friendly actors.
- Reticle built from the HUD's target bars, dynamic crosshair and Hardcore mode switches,
  optional sneak light and noise gauges, the RAD meter and ammo type on the HUD.
- HUD and Pip-Boy colour pickers (amber by default); smoother iron sights; a test version
  of 3D scopes (the zoomed world in the gun's lens) behind a switch.
- Reloading slows the player and NPCs to 75% (a Gameplay setting, on by default).

**Combat and AI**
- NPCs stay locked on a target in sight, hold fire when a wall blocks the shot, keep clear
  of walls in cover, throw the grenades they carry, use their stimpaks and re-arm with
  their best weapon.
- Creatures fight with their body weapons and switch by distance (Mister Handy's saw and
  flamer, Gutsy plasma and flamer); creature walks and runs measured against the original
  for every skeleton.
- Combat style data: dodges, rushes, combat radius and targeting; weapon choice, damage
  types and weapon mods from the game's data.
- Bloom under continuous fire and climbing recoil; sneak attack criticals; staggering.
- Dismemberment by blasts and NPC blows, with odds fitted to the original; explosions reach
  every body in their radius and only walls shield.
- VATS mode: hit chance per body part beside each mark, brighter marks, aiming down the
  sights while it runs, and kill cameras on the last kill of a run.
- Combat lines (OnFire, Poisoned, KnockedBack and the rest), warnings and trespass, idle
  chatter and reactions to the player.

**Perks and progression**
- A full perk audit against the master files, and most of the remaining perks built:
  attack and equip speed, VATS perks (Mysterious Stranger, Miss Fortune, Grim Reaper's
  Sprint …), melee knockdowns and unarmed special moves, Light Step, Pack Rat, Hoarder,
  Weapon Handling, companion perks and more.
- Hardcore needs at the original's rates; drowning with the O2 meter and Water Breathing.

**World and looks**
- The end credits: ExitGame rolls the credits over the loading screens.
- Destroyed objects with their replacement models and debris; swinging traps; water
  splashes from shots and explosions; corpses float.
- The Strip at night, Dead Money and Old World Blues lighting, the night sky and stars
  compared with the original; first-person sun shadows on by default with the flicker
  fixed.
- The B-29 rises out of Lake Mead; dust devils; particle emitters driven by models'
  animations.

**Followers and the world around you**
- Followers keep step, walk in a line and come through load doors with you; companion
  wheel commands take effect at the press; hired companions' kills give XP.
- Actors behind doors in loaded cells live by their packages; actors far away come over
  when heading for you; actors saved outside their cell come back on load.
- Compass quest markers lead through the shortest chain of load doors.

**Menus and the Pip-Boy**
- Pip-Boy: use stimpaks on one limb or the body, audio notes with a time meter, a moving
  radio wave, a weapon skill badge, notes taken as items filed in the Notes.
- Mod menu stays open, removes mods and shows their effect; crafting lists ready recipes
  first; Quick Save and Quick Load work.
- Terminals show lock difficulty and owned ones in red; hacking is a crime.

**Performance and robustness**
- Cells and actors are read ahead on worker threads; FaceGen tints moved into the shader;
  far fewer streaming hitches (Primm's 1.3 s freeze fixed).
- Broken or missing files never stop the game; a damaged archive is named at start;
  NIF, sound, animation and plugin readers hardened against corrupt data.
- The world is held behind every loading screen until cells, NPCs and scripts are ready.

**Scripts and saves**
- No script function is a stub any more; new events (OnOpen, OnClose, OnActorEquip,
  OnMagicEffectHit).
- Saves keep patrol points, sandbox days and players 2-4 for the coming co-op mode.
- Headless walkthroughs of the main quest and add-ons, with three end-game stalls fixed.

## Not built yet

Script extender support (xNVSE and JIP LN), power attacks and mines in VATS mode,
exterior cloud placement, and the rest of the gaps listed in `docs/outstanding.md` in the repository, ranked by what blocks a playthrough.
