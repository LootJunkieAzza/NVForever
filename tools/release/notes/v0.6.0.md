The sixth NVForever pre-release: everything merged since v0.5.1 (2026-10-29), about 270
pull requests. The main news is **script extender support built into the engine** (xNVSE,
JIP LN, JohnnyGuitar and a dozen smaller libraries, no DLLs), **Bevy 0.20 and a large
performance pass** (water reflections, shadows, LOD and loading), **the Tale of Two
Wastelands opening**, and many AI, combat, HUD and menu fixes checked against the original.

**You need your own copy of Fallout: New Vegas (1.4.0.525, base game + all DLC).** No game
data is included. Unpack anywhere outside the game folder and run `nvforever.exe` (or
`nvforever.cmd`; `./nvforever.sh` on Linux). The first start asks for your game folder and
remembers it, and the game opens on the main menu. `README-release.md` in the archive
explains the rest. NVForever never writes into the game folder or
`Documents\My Games\FalloutNV`; its own home is `Documents\My Games\NVForever`.

Script extenders: NVForever runs the script functions, syntax and events of xNVSE, JIP LN,
JohnnyGuitar and the libraries listed below as part of the engine. NVSE plugin `.dll` files
are never loaded, so a mod that relies on a plugin's engine patches, or on a function not
built yet, may still not work.

## New since v0.5.1

### Engine and performance
- Upgraded to **Bevy 0.20.1**; the vendored bevy_pbr is gone (#939). Unused Bevy plugins are switched off (#1405). Fixed a GPU memory leak in long sessions (#905).
- **Water reflection** camera runs only while water is on screen, judged by a GPU water-pixel count (Primm 51 → 80 fps), and can be turned off in settings (#1047, #1065, #1066, #1403).
- **Shadows**: sun cascades draw only the casters over their slice of the view, use 2048² maps, and the moon uses one cascade (#1371, #1418, #1419). The arms shade light is about 3 ms cheaper (#1397). Ambient occlusion runs at half resolution (#1402).
- **LOD**: object blocks and terrain chunks are built on worker threads, kept to a rebuild budget, and terrain no longer starves object blocks (#1044, #1052, #1121).
- **Loading and memory**: JIP record fields are decoded on first use (heap 507 → 207 MB). Script data loads in slices. Archives inflate outside the lock, and collision loading shares shapes between copies (#1105, #1123, #1153, #1360). Asset cache limits follow RAM and the GPU budget (#1169, #1218). Interior stalls are fixed (#1440).
- **Fewer per-frame costs** in physics hit volumes, the character controller, AI obstacle polls, animation, effect shaders, ripples, particles, the script runtime, the UI tree and sound decoding (#1099, #1321, #1343, #1347, #1431, #1437, #1445, #1446, #1449, #1489, #1490, #1492, and others).
- Game text is read in the install's own code page (1252, 1251 or 1250) (#1441). Files open whatever the case of their names (#1137). `SArchiveList` in NVForever.ini replaces the shipped base list (#1214).

### Gameplay and AI
- **Combat**: melee knockback (#1219). Attack clips that travel carry their actor, such as the Deathclaw lunge (#1209). Combat groups share alarms and targets (#1288). VATS hit chance is fitted to the original, and VATS has a kill camera (#1195, #1368). Explosions damage every limb (#1350). NPC weapon choice weighs explosives properly (#1304).
- **Movement**: player speed follows Speed Mult and armour (#1373). Falls hurt one leg and play the hard-landing sound (#1231). Jump and land footsteps play (#1215). Creatures turn at the data's rate (#1278). Coyotes warn and bite as they do in the original (#1190).
- **AI**: NPCs stop and face a load door before going through it (#1481). They are already at their package's place after a fast travel or a load door (#1269). New and fixed behaviour for Guard and Remain Near packages (#1202), Avoid Radiation (#1200), Observe Combat spectators running from a fight (#1216), the No AI Acquire flag (#1118), sandbox recovery after a fight (#1508), and leg-posing idles that NPCs break out of to walk (#1175).
- **Detection, crime and karma**: detection survives a save and load (#1265). Companions no longer set [DETECTED] (#1247). Unseen, silent targets no longer lower the detection value (#1150). Witnesses who ignore crime say the NoCrime topics (#1198). Killing an Evil character gives +100 karma (#1234).
- **Needs and Hardcore**: needs rise in whole points per waited hour, sleep heals Health, and Stimpak effects are fixed (#1178, #1271, #1442).
- **World**: dropped weapons fall at once and show in corpse loot (#1136, #1140). Destroyed car parts fall (#1413). Animated objects keep their collision, and gates left open reload open (#1433). Scripted moves of clutter stick (#1316). Script triggers follow moved references (#1141). A save made high up loads at its height (#1243).
- **Dialogue and voice**: Speech successes and failures are counted (#1298). Lines that plugins add follow their Previous INFO (#1199). Equal-priority topics are ordered newest learned first (#1205). Goodbye scripts can start the next conversation (#1249). Seated NPCs greet from their seat (#1480). Robots light up as they talk (#1201). Voices take the room's reverb (#1281). Template voices are fixed (#1116).
- **Quests**: ResetQuest and objective fixes (#1233, #1244). Five batches of headless walkthroughs, with their stalls fixed (#930, #933, #941, #1147, #1263, #1289, #1301).

### UI, HUD and menus
- **HUD**: corrected against captures from the original (bars, compass, radiation, enemy bar rule) (#902, #942). Notices are drawn from tiles and hidden behind menus. Fonts sit on per-font baselines (#952, #1040, #1135, #1166, #1236, #1245). A corner notice appears for new notes (#1259).
- **UI tile tree** (behind `[UI] TileMenus`): game strings table, pointer and keys, the remaining tile functions, and these menus drawn from their menu files: message box, quantity prompt, Wait/Sleep, tutorial (with a blurred background) and the pause menu's Mods page (#954, #957, #1005, #1045, #1223, #1224, #1307, #1308, #1493, #1495).
- **Pip-Boy**: full-screen menu option (#1229). In ITEMS, right click drops and controller buttons match the original (#1411). List clicks work on STATS and DATA (#1348), and layout is fixed (#1344, #1331).
- **Settings**: frame rate counter (#1095), crosshair style (#1097), variable refresh (G-Sync/FreeSync) (#1217), Invert Y and separate mouse and controller sensitivity (#1453), and controller aim assist (#1266). The page fits the view (#1260). Sound defaults match the original (#1456).
- Menus use their words from the game's strings (chargen name, reputation box, How many?, hacking) (#1182, #1188, #1313). Level-up music plays (#1193). The weapon wheel clicks (#1194). The reputation box and level-up menu wait until 2 s after a fight (#1207). The terminal screen has a new look (#1293).
