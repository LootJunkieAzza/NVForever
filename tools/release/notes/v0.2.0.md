The second NVForever pre-release: everything merged from v0.1.0 (2026-10-13) to 2026-10-18,
about 150 pull requests.

**You need your own copy of Fallout: New Vegas (1.4.0.525, base game + all DLC).** No game
data is included. Unpack anywhere outside the game folder and run `nvforever.cmd` (Windows)
or `./nvforever.sh` (Linux). The first start asks for your game folder and remembers it, and
the game opens on the main menu: Continue, New Game, Load Game, Settings, Quit.
`README-release.md` in the archive explains the rest. NVForever never writes into the game
folder or `Documents\My Games\FalloutNV`; its own home is `Documents\My Games\NVForever`.

## New since v0.1.0

**Starting and saving**
- The original's main menu, with its logo and theme. New Game plays the intro film, then the
  opening at Doc Mitchell's.
- Saved games: F5/F9, autosaves, and Save and Load on the pause menu, with thumbnails.
  Loading while playing goes through a loading screen.
- First start: a dialog for the game folder, NVForever.ini, and NVForever's own plugins.txt
  for mods.

**Character**
- NVForever's own character creator: race, sex, starting faces, the FaceGen sliders, hair,
  beards, brows and eyes. The same creator serves as the barber (hair and beard only) and the
  plastic surgeon (face only).
- Experience and levelling to the cap (30, plus 5 per story add-on), the HUD's XP meter, the
  level-up menu with skill points and the perks page, and Intense Training.

**Gameplay**
- **VATS mode**: NVForever's own slow-time mark-and-fire, after Red Dead Redemption's Dead
  Eye, in place of the original's VATS. Press V to slow time, click targets and body parts
  to mark them (each mark costs the weapon's AP), right click to take a mark back, then E to
  fire them all in slow motion. Single-shot pistols and rifles for now.
- Repair: vendors repair your gear for caps (ShowRepairMenu), and R on the Pip-Boy's ITEMS
  page repairs an item with a like one.
- Fast travel from the Pip-Boy's World Map.
- Casino games: blackjack and roulette as the original's, and NVForever's own Texas Hold'em
  poker.
- Crafting at workbenches, campfires and reloading benches. Wait and sleep refuse while
  falling, in combat or trespassing.

**NPCs and combat**
- Knock-outs: essential NPCs and those out of fatigue drop as ragdolls and get up again with
  the game's own clips. ResurrectActor stands corpses back up.
- NPCs draw what the rules equip them with, and move with a drawn weapon on that weapon's own
  clips.
- Melee combos, creature power attacks (the radscorpion's sting), blocking, crouching to
  fire, strafing, cover, and reloading with the weapon's clips.
- Crippled legs make people and creatures limp. Fleeing NPCs cower and hide crouched.
- Detection by and of the player, and fights between NPCs far from the player.
- NPC behaviour measured against the original game: how often NPCs talk to each other and
  call out in combat, and how close melee attackers stand.
- Talking gestures and fidgets (weapon and furniture), head tracking, feet on slopes and
  steps, and blended stance changes.
- Combat lines in the right context, fire and poison damage over time, and the death camera
  with a fade to black.

**Interface, sound and settings**
- The pause, settings and main menus in the original's look, with the game's mouse pointer.
- Settings: display (anti-aliasing choice, shadow filtering, FOV, vsync, frame cap,
  borderless window, draw distances), sound volumes by category, rebindable controls, and
  controller support with rumble and button pictures.
- The HUD's crippled-limb picture, enemy health bar, sneak word, compass marks and red
  crosshair. The terminal on the game's monitor model.
- The developer console's world, camera, info and quest commands.

**Scripting**
- Every script function the shipped scripts use was checked against the original game:
  281 probes run in the original. 42 functions remain stubs; they wait for systems not built
  yet.

## Not built yet

Live knockdowns (as opposed to knock-outs), the Caravan card game, slot machines, the
companion wheel, the tutorial menu, and credits. The full list, ranked by what blocks a
playthrough, is in `docs/outstanding.md` in the repository.
