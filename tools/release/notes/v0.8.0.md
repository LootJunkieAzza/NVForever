The eighth NVForever pre-release: everything merged since v0.7.0 (2026-11-11), about 420
pull requests. The main news is **the NVForever launcher** (news, settings, mods, and
updates that install themselves), **DLSS Super Resolution and Frame Generation**, **a
Tweaks tab with most of Stewie's Tweaks built in**, **smooth terrain**, **grass fires**,
**a settings menu overhaul**, **Advanced Movement**, **the Pip-Boy drawn from the game's own
menu files**, and a long list of AI, quest, combat and mod-support fixes checked against
the original.

**You need your own copy of Fallout: New Vegas (1.4.0.525, base game + all DLC).** No game
data is included. Unpack anywhere outside the game folder and run `nvforever-launcher.exe`
or `nvforever.exe` (or `nvforever.cmd`; `./nvforever.sh` on Linux). The first start asks
for your game folder and remembers it. `README-release.md` in the archive explains the
rest. NVForever never writes into the game folder or `Documents\My Games\FalloutNV`; its
own home is `Documents\My Games\NVForever`.

**Reports** stay opt-in: nothing is sent unless you chose **Send reports** at the first
start, and Settings → Gameplay → Reports turns them off or deletes what was sent. New in
this release, a form to send a report of a problem yourself, in your own words (#2154).

## New since v0.7.0

### Launcher
- **The NVForever launcher**: news, a release countdown, Play / Continue, the settings, the mods and your game folder in one window (#2712). Its Settings page follows the game's own menu (#2830, #2882).
- It **downloads, checks and installs new releases**, and can roll back to the one before (#2717).
- It sets up DLSS from the game's own download (#2769, #2919), and takes a start quest and intro film from the Mods page (#2947).

### Graphics and performance
- **DLSS Super Resolution** and **DLSS Frame Generation** through NVIDIA Streamline (Windows, Vulkan), with Reflex while frame generation runs; **Download DLSS…** in the settings fetches the files after you accept NVIDIA's licence (#2206, #2366, #2330, #2565, #2799, #2800). **NVIDIA low latency** mode on supported cards (#2224).
- **FSR** runs through a FidelityFX library in the game's folder, so OptiScaler can replace it (#2225).
- **Smooth terrain**: the landscape's jagged steps are smoothed for drawing and collision (Off, 2x2, 4x4, or 4x4 near the camera; on by default) (#2653, #2801, #2936). Optional **terrain anti-tiling** and **height-based blending** between textures (off by default) (#2709, #2668).
- **Sun shadows**: Off, Performance and Quality, three or four cascades, a Shadow resolution setting, and no more shimmer or steps as the sun moves (#2182, #2887, #2840, #2441).
- **Ambient occlusion** reworked: a ground-truth estimate, applied to ambient and lamp light only (#2978, #2989).
- **Experimental looks, off by default**: terrain shadows beyond the sun shadows' range, cloud shadows, desert haze, heat haze, and placed meshes blended into the ground (#2920, #2904, #2976, #2977, #2816).
- **Sky and colour grading** reworked: the Capital Wasteland's sky is no longer brown (TTW), and the Strip and Freeside skies match the original more closely (#2850, #2917). Eye adaptation no longer darkens daylight in steps (#2943). Under-water colour follows depth (the Pitt's bridge is no longer black) (#2957).
- **Faster frames**: terrain layers lit once, leaf cards cut and sorted, far shadow cascades updated less often, fewer GPU uploads, less MSAA work, music and radio read off the main thread, and small main-thread costs cut (#2181, #2187, #2184, #2178, #2267, #2283, #2292, #2719). No more hitch when the Vigor Tester first appears (#2740).
- Cards with little video memory hold less of it, and the game tells you when memory runs short (#2842).
- Weather fades turn back smoothly at a region's edge, and script-set weather carries over between worldspaces (#2702, #2779).
- Child worldspaces take their parent's land, water, climate and distant view (TTW's Megaton, Oasis, Paradise Falls) (#2950).

### Settings, HUD and menus
- **Settings menu overhaul**: categories, one column of values, lists instead of sliders for short choices, sharper menu text and notes that never overlap (#2804, #2497, #2813). Quality presets write NVForever.ini once (#2972).
- **A Tweaks tab**: most of Stewie's Tweaks built into the engine as settings, no mod needed (#1892).
- **Advanced Movement** (NVF Features tab): optional movement inertia, weapon handling, smoother first-person motion, and an optional sprint (#2811, #2967, #3002, #3003). **Quick Loot**: an optional loot list beside the crosshair (#2935).
- A **Physics rate** setting (60 to 160 Hz, or Match FPS), with smooth drawing between steps (#2371).
- HUD scale and Menu scale are separate (#2524); a viewmodel field of view slider (#2518, #2609); Vertical Sensitivity for mouse and controller (#2241); a Gore Shake setting (#2910).
- **The Pip-Boy is drawn from the game's own menu files by default**, the DATA page's Radio, World Map and Local Map included (#2307, #2208, #2213, #2199).
- Mod Configuration Menu pages show on the pause menu's Mods page (#2207, #2946).
- The performance overlay has an Upscaling row and says when CPU or GPU use can't be read (#2323, #2304). Alt+Enter switches between a window and full screen (#2219). The main menu greys out Continue and Load Game when there are no saves (#2302).
- **Controller**: switches with keyboard and mouse by itself (#2303); RB assigns hotkeys on the ITEMS pages (#2788); the pointer hides when unused (#2437); the stick and D-pad change S.P.E.C.I.A.L. (#2171, #2545); LT/RT and the D-pad in container and barter menus (#1827, #2214); tutorials scroll and show the button hints for your device (#2175, #2205); Back shows its hint (#2211).
- Classic V.A.T.S. names both target and body part, and the ammo shown leaves out rounds already queued (#2789).

### Combat and gameplay
- **Grass fires**: burning shots and explosions can set grass alight, and the fire spreads within a set limit (can be turned off) (#2572, #2682).
- **Sneaking**: loud surroundings cover your noise, and the noise gauge shows it (#2694).
- **God mode** also stops wear, radiation and script damage, and gives unlimited Action Points (#2767, #2805).
- Melee fighters and creatures keep up with you when you back away, so they can no longer be kited (#2168).
- Followers join their leader's fights, and the player's faction allies join the player's (House's Securitrons at Hoover Dam fight the Legion) (#2814, #2949).
- Attacking a Fiend alerts the other Fiends; lost enemies keep searching until their group is done (#2293, #2306). Very Aggressive creatures attack only inside their aggro radius (#2633).
- Gas traps ignite from shots, blasts and gunfire (Vault 22's spore purge) (#2695). Shots pass through chain-link fences; melee still stops on them (#2486). Explosions reach ragdolled corpses (#2489).
- Children never fight; companions are knocked out instead of killed outside Hardcore (#2540, #2639). Cornered NPCs with explosive weapons switch weapons (#2748). NPC semi-automatics fire at their animation's pace (#2818).
- A crime's grudge wears off as in the original (the Strip Securitrons, Karl at Red Rock) (#2498, #2506). Pickpocketing rolls afresh every time (#2773). The NCR disguise works again (#2654).
- NPCs with their weapon away play idle fidgets; guards wander inside their area; idle chatter is quieter (#2195, #2193, #2159). Drinking from sinks and pumps plays the animation (#2487).

### Script extenders and mods
- **Tested against real mod lists**, Begin Again (TTW) among them, with many more xNVSE, JIP LN, JohnnyGuitar and kNVSE functions and fixes: event handlers, control press and release events, NIF block functions, animation and camera overrides, temporary form copies, weather and light queries, menu XML math, MCM colour pickers and key input for scripts and MCM pages (#2276, #2336, #2359, #2410, #2824, #2821, and many others, most by jojokengly).
- **Mod Organizer 2 profiles** are merged without MO2 running, their mod archives mounted (#2960, #2961). Archives in the older version 103 format load, and a plugin's numbered archives (Plugin1.bsa …) load with it (#2243, #2523). Loose files in NVForever's own mods folder load over Data (#2681).
- Modern Minimap's map picture draws where its plugin would (#2963). Door scripts from mods run when an NPC walks through (#2196).
- For modders: an experimental in-game plugin editor and dialogue debugger (off unless turned on in NVForever.ini) (#2331).

### Linux
- The performance overlay shows CPU, GPU and memory use, memory budgets are read, and native crashes are written to the run log (#2107, #2115, #2304, #2301).

## Fixed since v0.7.0

### Combat
- Crashes on V.A.T.S. kills and from gore pieces fixed (#2368, #2204).
- The HUD ammo counter shows a Recharger weapon's charge; enemy health bar ticks fade (#2172, #2700, #2953).
- Scripted shooters (Use Weapon packages) keep firing in combat, face their target, keep their gun's rhythm, and a scripted sure hit lands (#2145, #2736, #2770, #2760). Turrets fire from where they stand (the ARCHIMEDES strike works) (#2635, #2618).
- NPC weapons show their moving parts; the missile launcher shows one missile per shot (#2646, #2509).
- Bodies thrown by scripts fly as they die; actors killed while getting up fall; failed ragdolls lie down; ragdoll limbs keep their limits (#2776, #2220, #2490, #2370).
- Resurrected actors get their gear back, so the reputation hit squads come armed (#2727). The HELIOS One stasis protectrons no longer notice you on arrival (#2329). Leaving V.A.T.S. with B no longer opens the Pip-Boy (#2444).

### AI and NPCs
- NPCs go back to what they were doing after an alert or a search, and script-added packages no longer drop after a few seconds (#2152, #2136, #2147).
- When a script moves the clock forward, NPCs pick up their new routine (no more night stalkers in Goodsprings on a new game's morning) (#2488, #2552).
- NPCs find their way beside locked doors and through open ones, reach places no door leads to, take safe drops, and face the right way on arrival (#2783, #2744, #2790, #2785).
- Scripted NPCs in unloaded areas still run their scripts (Chauncey comes to the steam room) (#2493). NPCs through a load door have one body (#2812). Seated NPCs stand up after a load (#2806). NPCs no longer react as you leave (#2932).
- Cachino finds you in Gomorrah (#2655). Vulpes no longer turns on you at the Fort (#2619). Templated actors use the right combat style (#2188). Scripts see a fight as the original did (#2189).
- Restrained NPCs can talk; NPCs talk to talking activators and through intercoms (Veronica at Hidden Valley) (#2399, #2476, #2479, #2817).
- Creatures stand still while they draw; Mister Handy's saw spins; Mr. House's screens never fight or swing between lines (#2691, #2648, #2237, #2516, #2752, #2153).
- No double left hands on hologram outfits and gloves; rigid headgear sits on the head; first-person sleeves for outfits without arms (#2342, #2647, #2684, #2174).

### Dialogue and menus
- A Goodbye line holds the conversation until it is said; the dialogue zoom stays on the speaker (#2158, #2305). A line already being said is no longer restarted (the Strip gate) (#2583).
- ED-E's greeting is said; faction-based speakers say their lines; walk-up greetings no longer pop up again (Orris); Curtis keeps talking into the radio (#2379, #2622, #2778, #2807).
- The repair and recipe menus open from a conversation, and their keys no longer also act on the ITEMS list (#2400, #2642).
- Item cards break long effect lists into lines and show ammo effects below weight and value (#2581, #2644). Pip-Boy STATS no longer counts apparel effects twice; mod art and map markers are scaled correctly (#2503, #2854). The hacking hover sound no longer stutters (#2234).

### Quests and scripts
- Hoover Dam: the control panel takes Yes Man's override chip, the Power Plant 01 elevator works, and quest targets several doors deep are marked right (#2161, #2640, #2539).
- Locked or disabled load doors no longer count as a way through (Nellis, Broken Steel); doors without their own lock use their partner's (REPCONN) (#2610, #2832, #2718).
- The Lucky 38 elevator's floor choice comes back; Mr. House's chamber stays sealed until the terminal opens it (#2500, #2510).
- Ramos's escort and the Hidden Valley intercom no longer soft-lock; the Boomers' barrage stops when you leave; Ibsen's virus purge timer runs out; Orris's ambush plays out; the Beyond the Beef arrest finishes; the Silver Rush intro's allies stay out (#2465, #2463, #2477, #2492, #2605, #2522).
- Anders at Cottonwood Cove can be freed, is found on his cross and his head hangs right (#2658, #2660). The add-on intros and end slides face the right way and change slides (#2236, #2726). A script's sleep during a trip passes behind the loading screen (#2288).
- Completed or failed quests stay stopped (#2959). Techatticup's hostages and the Black Mountain robot repair message work (#2969, #2968).
- Tale of Two Wastelands: Vault 101 and Megaton routines, shots, lines, switches and residents (#2239).

### World, graphics and sound
- A crash on Intel graphics when shaders are built is fixed (#2780). A lost graphics device ends the run cleanly (#2942).
- HELIOS One's plaza is solid; dozens of undiscoverable map markers can be found (#2815, #2483).
- Objects, doors and traps a script turns on appear in their pose and work (#2484, #2564). Indoor clutter settles; clutter on creased ground falls asleep; corpses no longer start under the terrain (#2369, #2970, #2973).
- No floating backpacks over the Fort, no white editor shapes at Red Rock, no getting stuck in caravan doors (#2546, #2504, #2505). Smoother over curbs and rocks (#2259).
- Glows and light sheets fade at their edges and face you; no glossy streak on Primm's sidewalk; the Stealth Boy's shimmer shows on every cloaked actor; dismembered bodies keep their stump (#2706, #2667, #2337, #2649, #2678).
- Mr. House's chamber effects animate; scrolling textures move on every shared part (the Hidden Valley storm); older BC5 normal maps and odd-sized weapon textures decode (#2787, #2480, #2620, #2165).
- Power armour has its heavy footsteps; stopped sounds fade instead of clicking and loud scenes no longer clip (#2554, #2238).

### Reports, logs and tools
- Closing the window is a clean exit; a game still running is no longer reported as a crash; slow loads and stuck controls are reported with their cause (#2204, #2262, #2730, #2784, #2367, #2837).
- Missing files the base game also lacks are no longer reported (#2287, #2606).
- Builds and checks: a release-quick profile, parallel CI jobs, the release workflow's token made read-only except for publishing, and cargo deny on the engine too (#2265, #2264, #2244, #2251, #2913).
