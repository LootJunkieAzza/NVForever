The fourth NVForever pre-release: everything merged since v0.3.0 (2026-10-18), about 130
pull requests. The main news is **Caravan, slot machines and the companion wheel**,
**dismemberment**, **weapon mods and scopes**, and a pass over the game's data that the
engine used to read and ignore.

**You need your own copy of Fallout: New Vegas (1.4.0.525, base game + all DLC).** No game
data is included. Unpack anywhere outside the game folder and run `nvforever.exe` (or
`nvforever.cmd`; `./nvforever.sh` on Linux). The first start asks for your game folder and
remembers it, and the game opens on the main menu. `README-release.md` in the archive
explains the rest. NVForever never writes into the game folder or
`Documents\My Games\FalloutNV`; its own home is `Documents\My Games\NVForever`.

## New since v0.3.0

**Casino and companions**
- Caravan: the bet, the deck builder, the game and the payout, in caps, NCR dollars or
  Legion coins.
- Slot machines, and the companion wheel with its eight commands (passive companions,
  sneaking with the player).

**Combat**
- Dismemberment: limbs severed and exploded by the weapon, crippling and overkill, with
  meat caps and lootable gore. Ash and goo piles from energy weapons' critical kills.
- VATS mode: bursts, melee (closing in within 5 m) and thrown weapons.
- Weapon mods: the mod menu, modded models in the world and in the hand. Scopes with their
  overlay, night vision and sway; true iron sights; silenced shots.
- Mines placed and thrown; traps and rigged shotguns; grab, carry and throw loose objects
  and corpses.
- NPCs: cover from the gunman, strafing, 10 s before giving up a search, power attacks by
  combat style, jumps over ledges and obstacles, swimming, crouched weapon clips, weapon
  choice by combat style and range, weapons wearing from use.
- The player can be knocked down; crippled limbs have their effects; blood on the screen
  when hurt.
- Difficulty scales damage and kill XP; perk entry points for damage, DT, spread and VATS
  AP; perks that change skills.

**World and looks**
- Impact decals (bullet holes, scorches, blood), depth of field, motion blur, refraction
  (heat haze, the Stealth Boy), moving flags and holograms, particle smokes and fires on
  placed models, effect-shader dissolves.
- Weather: weighted rolls from the region's list, rain and lightning with their sounds;
  warm distant fog by day.
- Environment radiation, irradiated water, the Geiger counter and RAD meter; explosion
  radiation zones and damaging water.
- Reverb by the acoustic space; spent cases ejected at the clip's eject key.

**Game data now used**
- Encounter zones set levels; NPCs and creatures carry their death items; vendors sell only
  what their services allow; quest markers only on targets whose conditions pass.
- Challenges count and reward, skill books are read from the inventory, holotapes play in
  the Pip-Boy's Notes, records' alternate textures are drawn.
- AI packages: Can't Open Doors, package topics and styles, patrol points.

**Scripts and saves**
- Two rounds of script function fixes; only 6 functions remain stubs (42 in v0.3.0).
- Saves load into a different load order.

## Not built yet

Credits, the VATS kill cameras, blasts and NPC melee dismembering, and the rest of the ESM
gap audit (combat style details, actor and cell flags, many sounds). The full list, ranked
by what blocks a playthrough, is in `docs/outstanding.md` in the repository.
