# ESM data the engine does nothing with (audit, 2026-10-25)

The owner asked for an up-to-date audit because the documentation had gone stale: what
ESM data the engine still does nothing with, and which docs no longer match the code.
Snapshot: origin/main 5558d63c (#717). Tags as in CLAUDE.md. This replaces the gap lists
of [esm-full-audit-2026-10-24](esm-full-audit-2026-10-24.md) (snapshot 9bb10a60, 212
commits earlier) and [esm-gap-audit-2026-10-21](esm-gap-audit-2026-10-21.md); their
methods and reasoning still hold.

## 1. Method

- `fnvtool esm fields --schema schemas/fnv` over the ten masters (FalloutNV.esm, the four
  story add-ons, GunRunnersArsenal.esm and the four packs): every field and flag bit the
  data actually sets, with non-zero counts [observed]. Fields that are zero in every master
  are left out here (they are the 10-24 audit's §4 mod-risk list, unchanged).
- Each record's non-zero fields were traced through the consumers on main (everything
  outside `crates/fnv-esm` and `crates/fnvtool`) by four readers: items and combat, actors
  and AI, logic and effects, world and audio. A field counts as used only if behaviour,
  drawing, sound or the interface depends on it; read into a struct nothing reads is
  **stored only**.
- The claims ranked M or higher, and the ones that contradicted a doc, were rechecked by
  hand. Two of the readers' claims did not survive and are not listed: CREA "Flies" is read
  (`engine/nvforever/src/residents.rs:555`), and LGTM lighting templates are applied in full
  through CELL LTMP (`crates/fnv-world/src/lighting.rs:84`).
- Ranks: **H** a player notices it in normal play, **M** it shows in places or in looks and
  sound, **L** minor or rare.

## 5. Docs that no longer match the code

All corrected in the same PR (2026-10-25): status.md and outstanding.md brought up to #717,
the sentences below fixed in their files, and weapon-mods.md §6 (projectile speed and ammo
regeneration were built). The 10-21, 10-23 and 10-24 audits carry a note pointing here.

Project-wide:
- [status.md](../status.md): headed 2026-10-17 and "#1–#417" (main is at #717); "205
  game-run scenarios" (527 now); "70 of 107 record types have layouts" (106 decode at least
  one subrecord through the schema; NAVI only by hand). Rows were updated piecemeal since.
- [outstanding.md](../outstanding.md): "as of PR #417"; its §2 rows are partly struck, partly
  not.
- [esm-full-audit-2026-10-24](esm-full-audit-2026-10-24.md): the gaps of §2 above, none
  struck; §5 "REGN RDMP read by nothing" (read, `read.rs:137`); §6 ASPC "no gaps" (the walla
  is stored only).
- [esm-gap-audit-2026-10-21](esm-gap-audit-2026-10-21.md) Visuals: "fog power and clip
  distance … all fog is linear" (both applied, `sky.rs:1562`, `:1632`).

Spec notes:
- object-fields.md §4: PROJ Supersonic and EXPL world orientation have consumers now (above).
- actor-fields.md §4: "VATS mode has no hit chance by design" (`fnv-combat vats.rs:507` uses
  BPTD To Hit Chance); "no IK system but head tracking" is half true (head tracking reads the
  IK start node and max angle).
- world-fields.md: WMI1 "no consumer" (read); NAVI "fnvtool only" (fnv-nav loads it, nothing
  reads it).
- world.md §6 (around line 375): sun glare and weather wind are built (`sky.rs:873`,
  `tree.rs:162`, `grass.rs:199`); only the wind's direction is a constant.
- cells-refs.md: "no cell uses Behave Like Exterior or XCCM" holds for FalloutNV.esm only.
- default-objects.md: the Meltdown explosion and the unarmed power-attack effects are built
  (`nvf-play combat.rs:1384`, `DefaultObject::Unarmed*`).
- aid-items.md (around line 336): "Not read: the other entry points" (built).
- logic-fields.md §4, dialogue.md: the Intelligence threshold and the speech-challenge
  settings exist in the master (§3 row 2, §4).
